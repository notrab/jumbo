# Watering Hole

Snapshots from the herd's favourite spot. Every image on this site is a single original in `public/photos`, resized, converted to WebP and compressed by Bunny Optimizer at the edge.

Images in Markdown become responsive images, with a `srcset` of Optimizer URLs:
